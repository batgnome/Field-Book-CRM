# Field Book CRM

A lightweight B2B customer relationship management system built to manage leads, customer accounts, contacts, contracts, and sales activity.

Field Book follows the customer lifecycle from initial lead acquisition through account conversion, contract negotiation, and ongoing contract management. It tracks sales activity and contract stage history to provide visibility into the sales process.

> 🚧 Field Book is currently in active development.

## Core Features

- Lead tracking and conversion into customer accounts
- Account and contact management
- Contract and subscription tracking
- Sales activity logging
- Contract stage history and audit trails
- User and salesperson assignment
- Shared address management
- Sales metrics and visualizations
- Report building
- Record archiving and soft deletion

## Tech Stack

- Python
- PostgreSQL
- Streamlit
- HTML/CSS

## Data Model

Field Book is centered around the B2B customer lifecycle:

**Lead → Account → Contact → Contract**

Supporting records track sales activity, addresses, contract stage changes, and record ownership.

## Project Goals

Field Book is a portfolio project focused on designing and implementing the kinds of workflows found in production business applications.

The project emphasizes:

- Relational database design
- Data integrity and foreign-key relationships
- Business workflow modeling
- Maintainable application architecture
- Historical and audit data
- Reporting and data visualization
